# Asset and dependency credits
Room Invaders gameplay scripts, three procedural monster designs, Pip, warning sounds, effects and music were developed for this project. Sounds and music were synthesized locally.

The project starts from Snap’s SPECS Base Template in Lens Studio 5.23.2. The render resources and occlusion support include template and toolkit assets. SimpleVertexBaseColor comes from the CLAD toolkit. See Assets/CLAD-APACHE-LICENSE.txt for the included Apache license.

Packages contains the installed Lens Studio packages used by the native project and its preview tests, including Spectacles Interaction Kit, Spectacles UIKit, LEAF, Bitmoji 3D and the preview interaction and inspection tools. Their existing package terms and notices apply. Lens Studio supplies the simulated room scenes separately.

The play and restart icons in Assets/Icons derive from Google Material Symbols, imported with CLAD IconSelector. Google distributes Material Symbols under Apache License 2.0. The planning interview used the Devpost Learn skill pack. The original skill pack and the private visual reference are not bundled here.
